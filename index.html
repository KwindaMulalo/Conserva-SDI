About Conserva

Conserva is South Africa's premier geoportal for protected area management, providing comprehensive geospatial tools and data services for conservation professionals, researchers, and policymakers.

Our platform integrates biodiversity data, and environmental monitoring systems to support evidence-based decision-making in conservation. We work closely with government agencies, academic institutions, and conservation organizations to ensure data accuracy and accessibility.

Our Services

Comprehensive geospatial solutions for conservation management and environmental monitoring.

Interactive Mapping

Access detailed protected area maps with multiple geospatial data layers — including CBAs, ESAs, MCAs, Marine Protected Areas, rivers, and more — all at your fingertips.

Data Analysis

Advanced analytical tools for spatial analysis, environmental monitoring, and conservation planning.

Data Repository

Centralized storage for conservation data with standardized formats and metadata management.

Data download

Download spatial data as Shapefile (.shp) — ready for use in QGIS, ArcGIS, or any GIS software.

Collaboration Tools

Share data and collaborate with partners across organizations with secure access controls.

Insights

Transform geospatial data into actionable conservation insights through advanced spatial analysis.
